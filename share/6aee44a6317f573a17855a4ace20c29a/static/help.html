Reading the case studies · 案例阅读指南

Explore three mathematical investigations through their goals, proof dependencies, alternative routes and recorded evidence. These pages preserve a published snapshot of historical research.

  1. Overview: read the exact question, result and main proof modules.
  2. Proof reader: open a step to inspect its premises, argument and source excerpts.
  3. Relation map: follow the main line, select a node to highlight its dependencies, and switch to all records to inspect alternative routes.
  4. Research history: inspect recorded branches, local obstacles and established facts.

Use the project selector to switch cases and 中文 / EN to switch the interface language. Mathematical source statements remain in their original language. Recorded evidence labels describe the checks performed during the research; browsing does not rerun them.

从目标到证据

先在「概览」了解原问题、结论和证明模块，再到「证明阅读」逐步展开前提、论证及原始来源。在「关系图」选择节点，可以突出它的依赖路径；切换「全部记录」可查看其他路线。「研究历史」保留研究分支、局部障碍和已有结论。

顶部可切换三个项目和中英文界面。数学原文保持原语言。这里展示的是历史研究快照，读者可以自由浏览；团队聊天、创建项目、恢复研究和模型调用均已关闭。
